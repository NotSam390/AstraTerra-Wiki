# Mars

The fourth planet from the Sun and home to the oldest off-world colonies in AstraTerra.

## Climate & Geography
Mars features vast iron-oxide deserts, canyon networks, and terraformed dome cities along the equator.

## Inhabitants
* **Martian Colonial Authority:** Governs the major biodomes.
* **Dustrunners:** Nomadic salvagers operating outside the domes.
